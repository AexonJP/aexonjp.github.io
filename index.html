          [07]FAQ

          Questions, answered.

          Anything else? Just ask — you’ll get a straight answer.

          What do you need from us to get started?

          A short call to understand your goals, plus access to your repository and hosting — or simply a description of your current setup. We come back with a plan, timeline and quote.

          Which CI/CD tools and hosting do you work with?

          Most often GitHub Actions and GitLab CI, deploying to Linux VPS servers and Docker hosts. Already using something else? We’ll work with what you have rather than force a migration.

          Can you take over an existing website or app?

          Yes. We start with an audit, fix whatever is urgent, then keep it updated, backed up and monitored on a Care plan.

          How is pricing decided?

          Every project is quoted from its scope after the first consultation, so you know the cost before any work starts. Care plans are billed monthly.

          Do you work remotely?

          Yes — we work remotely with clients and keep you updated at every stage, with progress you can see in your own repository.
